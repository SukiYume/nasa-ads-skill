# Research and Manuscript Workflows

Load this reference for reviews and manuscript introduction, methods, or discussion research. Apply the shared [research workflow](../SKILL.md#literature-research) and [reading scope](../SKILL.md#reading-scope). Use [literature-memory.md](literature-memory.md) for library operations.

## Start with the Research Question

State the scientific question, population or physical regime, time range, required evidence, and intended manuscript section. Search existing summaries, facets, methods, entities, and findings first. Reuse verified local versions and cached citations. Search ADS for missing coverage, newly published work, updated versions, and citation relationships.

Choose the candidate pool and refined investigation set using those criteria. Track selections and exclusions so the scope of the synthesis and any unfinished reading remain traceable. Source briefs, complete digests, and scientific interpretation retain their respective evidence levels.

## Review

Choose query families covering the terminology, physical mechanisms, observations, methods, and competing explanations relevant to the review. Record queries, filters, dates, pagination, screening rules, and the searched source. Use `runs` when checking ADS retrieval counts and summary completion.

Include foundational, representative, contradictory, and method-defining work within the requested scope. Compare questions, samples, data, methods, findings, uncertainties, limitations, and exact versions; an evidence matrix can help organize substantial comparisons. Group the synthesis around scientific questions and explain agreement, disagreement, and coverage gaps with traceable evidence. A comprehensive or systematic review requires an explicit coverage and screening protocol.

## Introduction

Assign the `intro` role. Retrieve the foundational results, current observational or theoretical state, competing explanations, and the specific unresolved question addressed by the manuscript. Check primary papers behind decisive historical or quantitative claims. Connect each intended claim to evidence and identify the scope over which it holds.

## Methods

Assign the `methods` role. Retrieve original algorithms, calibration and preprocessing procedures, estimator definitions, assumptions, validation studies, and known failure conditions. Inspect equations, units, parameter choices, selection functions, and implementation details in the stored artifact. Compare the proposed use with the conditions validated in the cited work. Record reusable method facets and their evidence locators.

## Discussion

Assign the `discussion` or `comparison` role. Compare samples, observational coverage, preprocessing, estimators, uncertainties, and selection effects before comparing reported values. Separate measurements, author interpretations, model-dependent predictions, and the present manuscript's inference. Retrieve contrary evidence and state unresolved differences. Use complete article records for detailed methodological or quantitative comparisons.

## Organization and Handoff

Use existing hierarchical scientific collections and applicable manuscript roles. Collection descriptions hold curated topic summaries. Notes preserve the connection between a paper and a particular manuscript or decision. Keep bibliographic metadata and article-derived evidence distinct from personal interpretation.

Use the shared [completion and handoff](../SKILL.md#completion-and-handoff) criteria. Export citations through the [local citation workflow](literature-memory.md#citation-export) when needed, and use `adslib` for requested Web browsing.
